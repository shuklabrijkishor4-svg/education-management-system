FAQ

Good to know before you register

How do I get my enrollment number?

It's generated automatically the moment your registration form is submitted successfully — no waiting period.

Can I log in with either my email or enrollment number?

Yes. The login page accepts your enrollment number or the email address you registered with, alongside your password.

Can I change my course after registering?

Yes, from your Profile page you can update your course selection and it saves directly to your record.

How do I delete my account?

Go to your Dashboard or Profile page and choose Delete Account. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is removed.
